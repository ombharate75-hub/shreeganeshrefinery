Our family legacy

Built across generations. Carried forward with trust.

Shree Ganesh Refinery & Touch has roots in our family’s gold and silver trade founded in 1968 by our late Nivrutti Ramchandra Bharate (Kaka). The business was then carried forward by our late father, Macchindra Nivrutti Bharate (Appa), and is now being continued by the third generation. Their example taught us that a good name is earned through honesty, loyalty, responsibility and keeping a customer’s trust. We honour their legacy by continuing to serve the jewellery trade with care, respect and transparent communication.
